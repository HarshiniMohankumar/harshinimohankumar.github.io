Case study 01 · Senior Product Manager · 2020 to 2023

Decathlon Play

A two-sided B2B2C marketplace connecting people who want to play sport with coaches, facilities and event organisers. I was the sole PM, working across web, Android, iOS and partner tooling.

B2C marketplaceMobile-firstConversionDiscoveryAnalytics
Mobile web: quick discovery and booking for users arriving through search, QR codes or shared links.
THE FRICTION

A fragmented sports ecosystem

People struggled to discover and book sport in one place. Partners lacked a unified way to reach customers, while growth depended too heavily on paid acquisition.

THE BET

Make discovery and booking effortless

I prioritised mobile, simplified the end-to-end booking journey and connected digital discovery with Decathlon’s physical store network.

WHAT CHANGED

Build engagement into the journey

Personalisation, in-store QR entry points, simpler sign-up, group sharing, ratings, reviews and in-app messaging made the experience easier to discover, trust and use.

Case study 02 · Product Manager · 2018 to 2020

Partner Platform & Automation

The supply-side engine behind the marketplace: a shareable self-service onboarding journey for coaches, event organisers, venues and facility providers. Instead of relying on a person to pitch and guide every partner, the website explains the value of joining Decathlon and lets partners start onboarding themselves.

0→1 platformPartner onboardingKYCAutomationOperations
THE FRICTION

Six weeks to say yes

Onboarding was manual and dependency-heavy. Operations had to hand-hold partners through paperwork, verification and launch.

DISCOVERY

Separate judgment from repetition

I mapped the journey and seven manual decision points, then identified which decisions truly needed people and which could be automated safely.

THE SOLUTION

Self-service with guardrails

A shareable onboarding website handled the pitch, eligibility flow and first steps. Rules-based KYC, fraud scoring and document validation then automated repeatable decisions, while people focused on exceptions and escalations.

How I work

From fuzzy problem to measurable outcome.

A simple product loop: understand the problem deeply, define what success means, build with the team, measure behaviour and keep improving.

01

Discover

User and partner interviews, journey mapping, behavioural data and the repeated question: “why?”

02

Define

Frame the problem, prioritise opportunities and connect product KPIs to business outcomes.

03

Build

Work iteratively with engineering, design and QA. Test before committing where possible.

04

Measure

Instrument the journey, review funnels and cohorts, and understand what changed, not just whether a number moved.

05

Improve

Feed post-launch insight back into the roadmap and keep removing friction.
