Case study 01 · Senior Product Manager · 2020 to 2023

Decathlon Play

A two-sided B2B2C marketplace helping people discover, learn and play sport through events, coaching, playgrounds and community-led experiences. I was the sole PM, working across web, Android, iOS, partner management app and onboaridng website.

Two-Sided MarketplaceProduct Vision & StrategyDiscoveryConversionAnalyticsPersonalization
Mobile web: quick discovery and booking for users arriving through search, QR codes or shared links.
THE FRICTION

A fragmented sports ecosystem

People lacked a single place to discover and book opportunities to learn, play and participate in sport. Partners had no unified way to reach customers.

THE APPROACH

Make discovery and booking effortless

I prioritised a mobile-first experience, simplified the booking journey and focused the roadmap on better discovery, trust and conversion.

WHAT CHANGED

From fragmented discovery to one connected journey

Users could discover, compare and book sport opportunities in one place, with simpler sign-up, personalised discovery and stronger trust through ratings, reviews and sharing.

Case study 02 · Product Manager · 2018 to 2020

Partner Platform & Automation

The supply-side platform behind Decathlon Play, designed to simplify partner onboarding and empower coaches, event organisers and facility providers to independently manage and grow their sporting offerings through self-service tools and automation.

0→1 platformB2B PlatformKYC & ComplianceWorkflow AutomationSupply-Side Growth
THE FRICTION

A bottleneck to marketplace growth

Onboarding was manual and dependency-heavy. Extensive paperwork and verification created operational bottlenecks, delaying partner activation and limiting marketplace growth.

DISCOVERY

Separate judgment from repetition

I mapped the journey and seven manual decision points, then identified which decisions truly needed people and which could be automated safely.

THE SOLUTION

Self-service with guardrails

I introduced self-service onboarding and automated KYC, fraud scoring and document validation, reserving human intervention for exceptions and escalations.
